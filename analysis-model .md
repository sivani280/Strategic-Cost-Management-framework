# Analysis Model
## Case Definiation
A manufacturing company depends heavily on a single supplier for a critical production material.The supplier continuously increase the material price,resulting in
increasing manufacturing costs and pressure on product margins
## Initial Questions
How dependent is the company on the primary supplier?
How does a supplier price increase affect material cost?
How does the material cost change affect manufacturing cost?
What happens if the company introduces another supplier?
What cost or risk trade-offs arise from supplier diversification?
